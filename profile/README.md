## MetaT8

Continuity infrastructure for mental health. A week has 168 hours and a therapy session takes one; MetaT8 holds the other 167, for patients and their clinicians.

- A patient mobile app: written and voice journaling, AI-supported reflection between sessions, homework from the clinician.
- A clinician dashboard: each patient's homework and short AI-written summaries, ready before the next session.
